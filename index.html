ThinkPilot

An Adaptive AI Framework for Programming Education

Developing a Python-based framework that analyzes student interactions and adapts AI assistance from conceptual hints and Socratic questions to guided or direct support. The system uses interaction logic, validation, logging, and multi-phase experimental workflows to study AI-supported programming education while preserving student autonomy.
